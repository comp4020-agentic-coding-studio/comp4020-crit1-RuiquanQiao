No script, anywhere

The feed is read when the site is built, never in your browser. Nothing on any page here is a script, which is the week's brief and also why the clock cannot tick.

The grid is computed

The build breaks every line to forty columns and counts the rows a page needs, so the page is fitted to your screen instead of scrolling off the bottom of it. Teletext never scrolled either.

When the feed is down

A committed snapshot is served instead. The site rebuilds on every push, so a feed that will not answer would otherwise take it down: a stale page is a teletext page, a failed build is nothing at all.

The clock

It says when the feed was read, not what time it is now. Times are Canberra's, converted from a British source.
